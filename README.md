

# 🏏 IPL Player Performance Dashboard (2008–2026)

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive IPL Player Performance Dashboard developed using **Microsoft Excel** to analyze and visualize player statistics across IPL seasons from 2008 to 
2026. The dashboard provides insights into individual player performances, team statistics, and overall IPL trends through interactive visualizations.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze IPL player performance across seasons.

- Identify top-performing batsmen and bowlers.

- Compare team and player statistics.

- Understand player roles and performance trends.

- Present cricket data through an interactive dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel

- Pivot Tables

- Pivot Charts

- Slicers

- Excel Formulas

- Data Cleaning

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Runs

- Total Wickets

- Total Matches

- Batting Average

- Strike Rate

- Bowling Economy

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- Top run scorers and wicket takers.

- Player performance analysis.

- Team-wise performance comparison.

- Player role distribution.

- Season-wise performance trends.

- Interactive filters and slicers.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations – Chart-by-Chart Analysis

1. Top 10 Run Scorers

Chart Type: Bar Chart

Purpose: Identifies the leading run scorers in IPL history.

Key Insights:

Virat Kohli leads with 9,336 runs.

Rohit Sharma ranks second with 7,046 runs.

Shikhar Dhawan follows with 6,769 runs.

David Warner has scored 6,565 runs.

KL Rahul has scored 5,815 runs.

Business Interpretation:

The chart highlights the importance of consistent batting performance and identifies players who have made significant contributions through runs.

Recommendations:

Prioritise consistent run scorers during team selection.

Compare total runs with batting average and strike rate.

Analyse player performance to support auction and squad planning decisions.

2. Top 10 Wicket Takers

Chart Type: Bar Chart

Purpose: Identifies players who have taken the highest number of wickets in IPL history.

Key Insights:

Yuzvendra Chahal leads with 233 wickets.

Bhuvneshwar Kumar follows with 226 wickets.

Sunil Narine and Jasprit Bumrah have taken 207 wickets each.

Piyush Chawla has taken 192 wickets.

Ravichandran Ashwin has taken 187 wickets.

Business Interpretation:

The chart highlights the contribution of experienced bowlers and their ability to take wickets throughout IPL seasons.

Recommendations:

Evaluate bowlers using both wickets and economy rate.

Identify consistent wicket-taking performers.

Analyse bowling performance during powerplay, middle overs and death overs.

3. Players Debut Trend (2008–2026)

Chart Type: Line Chart

Purpose: Displays the number of players grouped by their IPL debut year.

Key Insights:

2008 recorded the highest player count with 180.

2009 recorded 67 players.

2010 recorded 57 players.

2014 recorded the lowest count with 25 players.

2023 and 2026 recorded 58 players each.

Business Interpretation:

The chart helps understand the distribution of player debut years and how player representation varies across different IPL generations.

Recommendations:

Analyse player career longevity.

Compare experienced players with recently debuted players.

Identify emerging players based on recent debut years.

Evaluate performance across different debut-year groups.

Note: This chart represents player records grouped by debut year, not necessarily the number of new players entering the tournament in each season.

4. Players by Role

Chart Type: Doughnut Chart

Purpose: Displays the distribution of IPL players according to their playing roles.

Key Insights:

Bowlers represent the largest category.

All-rounders are the second-largest category.

Wicketkeepers represent the smallest category.

Business Interpretation:

The chart provides an overview of the player composition and helps understand the distribution of different playing roles.

Recommendations:

Maintain a balanced squad across different roles.

Identify available backup players for each role.

Consider player versatility during team selection.

Analyse role distribution when planning squad requirements.

5. Average Strike Rate by Role

Chart Type: Bar Chart

Purpose: Compares average batting strike rates across different player roles.

Key Insights:

Wicketkeepers have the highest average strike rate at 95.91.

Batters follow with 90.19.

All-rounders have an average of 84.48.

Bowlers have the lowest average at 46.66.

Business Interpretation:

The chart indicates differences in batting performance across player roles. Wicketkeepers and specialist batters have higher average strike rates than bowlers in this dataset.

Recommendations:

Evaluate batting strike rate when selecting aggressive players.

Identify bowlers who can contribute runs in lower batting positions.

Compare strike rate with batting average to understand scoring speed and consistency.

Consider individual player performance rather than relying only on role averages.

Note: Role-level averages should be interpreted carefully because players have different batting opportunities and match participation.

💡 Overall Business Insights

The dashboard provides the following important findings:

Batting Performance: Virat Kohli is the leading run scorer, demonstrating significant long-term batting contribution.

Bowling Performance: Yuzvendra Chahal leads the wicket-taking statistics.

Player Distribution: Bowlers form the largest player category in the dataset.

Debut Analysis: The inaugural IPL year, 2008, has the highest number of player records.

Strike Rate Analysis: Wicketkeepers have the highest average strike rate among the four player roles.

🚀 Recommendations and Future Improvements

Based on the analysis, the following improvements can make the dashboard more useful:

Team-wise Analysis: Compare runs and wickets across IPL franchises.

Country-wise Analysis: Analyse player contributions by country.

Player Efficiency: Compare batting average, strike rate and economy rate.

Season-wise Performance: Track individual player performance across different seasons.

Bowling Efficiency: Compare wickets with economy rate.

Advanced KPIs: Introduce average runs per match and wickets per match.
